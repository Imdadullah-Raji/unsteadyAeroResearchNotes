## Glauert Integral Problem

 Numerical solution of glauert integral blows up as steps are increased. 

$$\int_0^L \frac{\gamma(\xi)}{x-\xi} d\xi = -2\pi\alpha Q_\infty$$
Near the leading edge, circulation strength per unit length $\gamma$ tends to infinity as $d\xi \to 0$ .

The picture is way more complicated, the eqn above does not seem to have a unique solution. So, choosing different test values at $x$ produces vastly different results.
