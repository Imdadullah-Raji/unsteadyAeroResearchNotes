## Meshing in Cylinder Flow
First cell thickness should be determined by BL thickness.
For laminar flow Blasius BL thickness formula is $$\delta_{99} = \frac{5.0D}{\sqrt{\text{Re}}}$$
The wake refinement should be informed by vortex core radius. Vortex core radius actually depends on the definition. 
**Blockage Ratio**: in wind tunnels blockage ration is kept under 5%. 
**Shedding Wavelength**: For flow past a cylinder shedding wavelength is approximately 5D. The domain should cover 2-4 shedding wavelengths.
**Shedding frequency**:
Strouhal number $St = fU_\infty/D$  is function of $\text{Re}$ For flow past a cylinder it is almost constant $\text{St} \approx 0.17$ at $\text{Re}=100$. At Re = 100 and D = 1.0 m with U=1.0 m/s $f = 0.17 \text{Hz}$

To capture 5 shedding cycles we need to simulate for $T=5/0.17 =30 \;\text{s}$
